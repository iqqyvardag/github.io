Evidence confidence

Confidence belongs to a conclusion, not an ingredient.

The same omega-3 source may have stronger evidence for one defined outcome and weak or conflicting evidence for another.

Higher / moderate confidence

More consistent and directly applicable

Multiple suitable sources point in a similar direction, with fewer serious limitations. “Higher” never means absolute certainty.

Limited confidence

Promising but incomplete

Evidence may be small, indirect, inconsistent, imprecise or dependent on surrogate outcomes.

Mixed / uncertain

Results conflict or the question remains unresolved

Differences in formulation, population, dose, endpoint or study quality may prevent a single stable conclusion.

What confidence is not

It is not a product score, safety certification, probability of personal benefit or substitute for professional guidance.
