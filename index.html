The omega-3 family

One name.
Several distinct questions.

“Omega-3” can refer to a plant-derived essential fatty acid, marine long-chain fatty acids, foods, supplements or prescription medicines. Reliable interpretation starts by naming exactly which one is under discussion.

Evidence review

One question.
One defined conclusion.

Broad claims hide important differences. Our reviews narrow the question before judging the evidence.

High confidenceDefined conclusion

Do prescription omega-3 medicines lower fasting triglycerides in adults with elevated levels?

At 4 g/day, prescription omega-3 formulations lower triglycerides in adults with elevated levels. The size of effect varies with baseline triglycerides and formulation. This conclusion does not establish that ordinary retail supplements prevent cardiovascular events.

Population
Adults with elevated triglycerides
Intervention
Prescription omega-3 at 4 g/day
Outcome
Fasting triglycerides
