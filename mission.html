Mission & principles

Make omega-3 evidence understandable, transparent and useful.

So people can make better-informed health decisions without marketing pressure, false certainty or unnecessary complexity.

Omega-3 research is extensive, but it is not always easy to interpret. Findings can differ by population, dietary pattern, EPA and DHA dose, formulation, study duration and outcome. Headlines often remove those distinctions, while marketing may present uncertainty as certainty.

Our principles

Evidence before marketing

We do not shape conclusions to fit a commercial objective.

Transparency over appearance

We show sources, methods, dates, review status, conflicts and material corrections.

Clarity without distortion

We simplify language without removing context that changes meaning.

Uncertainty is information

We explain conflicting evidence, limitations and unanswered questions rather than hiding them.

Specific claims, not ingredient verdicts

We assess defined questions and outcomes. We do not label omega-3 universally effective or ineffective.

Food, supplements and medicines are different

We do not transfer findings between them without evidence.

No borrowed authority

Credentials, partnerships, testing and review are stated only when real, documented and relevant.

Safety belongs in the main content

Care boundaries and material risks are not buried in fine print.
