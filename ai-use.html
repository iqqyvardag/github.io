AI use

AI assistance does not replace source verification or accountability.

Human editors remain responsible for every published statement, citation, calculation and conclusion.

TriOrigin may use approved AI systems for drafting support, structure, language editing, coding assistance or internal research organization. AI output is not treated as evidence and is not published as an automatically generated scientific summary.

Required controls

  • Verify factual and numerical claims against suitable original or authoritative sources.
  • Check every citation; fabricated or mismatched references are prohibited.
  • Do not upload confidential manuscripts, personal health information or restricted data to unapproved systems.
  • Disclose material AI assistance when it affected content generation or analysis beyond routine spelling, grammar or formatting.
  • Maintain human editorial accountability and records sufficient to reproduce the verification process.

Beta disclosure

The initial website source and starter copy were produced with AI-assisted drafting and coding under human direction. Health statements were anchored to cited authoritative sources, but specialist review remains necessary before representing the beta as a complete scientific publication program.
