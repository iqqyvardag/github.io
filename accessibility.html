Accessibility

Evidence is useful only when people can reach and understand it.

TriOrigin targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA and treats responsive and accessible behavior as part of every component.

The beta includes semantic headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus styles, a skip link, labeled form inputs, text alternatives for meaningful visuals, reduced-motion support and color-independent status labels.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Report a barrier by emailing info@trioriginlabs.com with the page, device, browser and problem encountered.
