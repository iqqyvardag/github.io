Corrections

Errors should be corrected without concealment.

How to report a possible error and how TriOrigin evaluates, corrects and documents material changes.

TriOrigin LabsLast assessed 17 July 20266 min read

On this page

Report an errorAssessmentTypes of changeArchiving

Scientific communication can contain mistakes or become outdated. TriOrigin distinguishes routine updates, clarifications and material corrections, and aims to make meaningful changes visible.

Report a possible error

Send the page URL, the passage in question, the reason for concern and any supporting source to info@trioriginlabs.com. Do not include confidential medical information.

How reports are assessed

A report is checked against the cited source and, where necessary, original or authoritative material. Reporting a concern does not automatically mean an error has occurred, but credible concerns should be investigated.

Types of change

Routine update

New link, refreshed date, minor wording or additional context that does not change the conclusion.

Clarification

Wording is improved to prevent a material misunderstanding while the underlying conclusion remains unchanged.

Material correction

A factual or interpretive error changes an important statement, number, scope or conclusion. The page should carry a dated correction note.

Removal and archiving

When content cannot be safely corrected immediately, it may be temporarily unavailable. Superseded pages may be archived with a clear route to the current version rather than silently disappearing.
