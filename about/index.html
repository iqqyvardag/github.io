About

Evidence before marketing.

TriOrigin exists to make omega-3 research easier to understand without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.

Mission

Make omega-3 evidence understandable, transparent and useful.

TriOrigin Labs is an evidence-communication and public-education platform operated by TriOrigin LLC. We explain external research, organize evidence and make important limitations visible.

“Labs” is part of the brand name. It does not mean TriOrigin currently owns a laboratory, performs diagnostic testing or conducts original clinical research.

01

Evidence before marketing

Conclusions are not written to fit a future commercial objective.

02

Specificity over slogans

Food, supplements and prescription medicines are not treated as interchangeable.

03

Uncertainty made visible

Mixed, limited and indirect evidence is described plainly.

04

Corrections without concealment

Material errors are assessed, corrected and documented.
