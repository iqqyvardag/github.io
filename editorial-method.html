Editorial method

How TriOrigin forms and maintains a conclusion.

A visible workflow for defined questions, source checking, evidence synthesis, review, uncertainty and correction.

1. Define the question

Where relevant, specify population, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome and timeframe. Broad ingredient verdicts are divided into answerable questions.

2. Plan the sources

Core health conclusions prioritize original trials, official guidance, regulatory documents and high-quality systematic syntheses. Brand blogs, retailer pages and unsourced summaries do not establish a conclusion.

3. Verify the original material

Numbers, primary outcomes, corrections, retractions, funding and conflicts are checked against the original source whenever possible.

4. Separate evidence types

Mechanistic plausibility, observational association, biomarker change and clinical outcome are not presented as equivalent.

5. Assign confidence to a specific conclusion

Confidence considers consistency, directness, risk of bias, precision, applicability and the completeness of the evidence base.

6. State review status

A page identifies whether it has editorial review, specialist review or remains a beta publication awaiting additional review.

7. Publish limitations and care boundaries

Uncertainty and safety context appear in the main page, not only in a general disclaimer.

8. Update openly

Material corrections and evidence-driven changes are dated and explained. A conclusion is not silently rewritten.
