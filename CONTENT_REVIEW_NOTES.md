# Content Review Notes

The starter content is grounded primarily in the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements omega-3 fact sheet, the American Heart Association science advisory on hypertriglyceridemia, original human pharmacokinetic studies and the GOED voluntary quality monograph.

Before TriOrigin describes the clinical or safety pages as externally reviewed, a named qualified nutrition or clinical reviewer should check the claims, citations, boundaries and confidence assignment. The current evidence page transparently states that it is a focused evidence translation rather than a new systematic review conducted by TriOrigin.
